THE SALTLINE — light the beacon, hold the shore the tide redraws. A coastal tower defense: keep the beacon burning at the tip of a peninsula while raiders come in from the sea. After every wave the tide redraws the shoreline — the raiders' route and your buildable ground change — and the next tide line is shown before it arrives. At low tide, driftwood left on the new land becomes barricades and lantern towers. Survive 10 waves; if the beacon's flame dies, the shore is lost.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
